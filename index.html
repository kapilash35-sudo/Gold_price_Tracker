Sri Lanka Gold Market FAQ & Reference Guide

Why is 44,375 LKR the key benchmark rate?

44,375 LKR represents the official 24K pure gold per-gram selling benchmark on CMB Gold and matches international spot bullion converted into Sri Lankan Rupees (355,000 LKR per 8g sovereign).

How much is 1 Pawn / Sovereign?

In Sri Lanka, 1 Pawn (also called a Sovereign) equals exactly 8.00 grams. At 44,375 LKR/g, 1 sovereign of 24K pure gold is exactly 355,000 LKR ($44,375 \times 8$).

What does Midnight Close Rate signify?

Midnight closing prices from London and New York global bullion markets establish the opening reference baseline for Sri Lankan digital gold platforms and Sea Street jewelers the following morning.

How does CMB Gold source its rates?

CMBGOLD connects physical vaulted gold with live international market feeds and daily Sri Lankan bank exchange rates, quoting transparent buying and selling prices across 24K, 22K, 21K, and 18K.
